About Us

ERC – E CONOMICAL, RENTABLE COMMUNITY

ERC Communities specializes in developing vibrant neighborhoods of affordable, rentable single family homes, comprised of detached manufactured homes. Our residences redefine modern living, offering unparalleled comfort and style that exceed expectations, truly making you feel at home from the moment you step inside.
Gone are the days of outdated stereotypes associated with manufactured homes. Our properties boast contemporary designs featuring stunning kitchens equipped with upscale countertops, cabinetry, appliances, and lighting. With ample closet space, separate laundry rooms, and an abundance of natural light, our homes are designed for both functionality and aesthetic appeal. The spacious open floor plans, typically with three bedrooms, provide ample room (3BR and 2BA, approximating 1,400 sq ft) for families to thrive.
With affordable housing being one of the single most pressing challenges for growing families, and consumer preference shifting away from apartments, ERC community is the perfect alternative to expensive build to rent overpriced developments.
At ERC Communities, we prioritize the convenience and satisfaction of our residents. Our commitment to responsive customer service, an intuitive online tenant portal, and efficient maintenance requests ensures that your living experience remains hassle-free. Additionally, we offer lawn service for all tenants, further enhancing the ease of living within our communities.

EXCITING, RELAXING AND CONNECTED

We are community that offers a dynamic range of activities and events to keep families engaged and entertained. At the same time, it provides spaces and opportunities for relaxation and unwinding, fostering a sense of calm and tranquility. Through various social gatherings and shared experiences, members of this community feel deeply connected to each other, forming bonds that enrich their lives and create a supportive network for all.

Locations

Located in Zephyrhills, our flagship community features communal green space where residents can relax, play, and socialize amidst lush greenery. With ample space between homes, families can enjoy privacy without compromising on the sense of community.
If you’re tired of the cramped confines and noisy neighbors associated with overpriced city dwellings and shared walls, ERC Communities offers a compelling alternative. For the price of a small apartment, you can rent a spacious, new three-bedroom, two-bathroom home with a generous yard in a welcoming neighborhood. Experience the difference of modern, affordable living with ERC Communities.
ERC Communities strategically focuses on developing properties in high-migration locations across the Southeast corridor. Our meticulous selection process ensures that our communities are situated conveniently outside major metropolitan areas, such as Tampa with our flagship Zephyrhills location. This deliberate choice offers residents the best of both worlds: the tranquility and spaciousness of suburban living while maintaining easy access to the amenities and opportunities of growing cities.
By choosing carefully selected locations, ERC Communities provides residents with more than just a home; we offer a lifestyle tailored to modern needs. Enjoy the serenity of expansive green spaces and the sense of community fostered by our thoughtfully designed neighborhoods. And when the allure of city life beckons, effortless access to nearby urban centers ensures that entertainment, employment, and cultural opportunities are always within reach.
Experience the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and affordability with ERC Communities. Welcome home to a better way of living.
